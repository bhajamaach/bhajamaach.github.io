Instead of fitting a known equation to data, Niyom tries to find the equation itself — using SINDy (Sparse Identification of Nonlinear Dynamics) and LASSO-based sparse regression to pull out the governing physics law directly from raw measurements.

Datasets and experiments are defined through a custom `.niy` file format, paired with a Tauri desktop dashboard for visualizing whatever equation falls out.
